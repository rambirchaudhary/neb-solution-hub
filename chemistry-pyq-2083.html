Group A — Multiple Choice Questions

Attempt all questions. [11×1=11]

  1. Which order of reaction does the following chemical equation represent? RCOOR + H₂O --(H⁺)--> RCOOH + ROH
    • A) Zero order
    • B) Pseudo-first order
    • C) Second order
    • D) Third order
  2. What is the normality of 5% Na₂CO₃ solution?
    • A) 0.94 N
    • B) 0.98 N
    • C) 0.99 N
    • D) 1.01 N
  3. Which of the following is the ionic product of water at 393 K?
    • A) Greater than 1×10⁻¹⁴
    • B) Less than 1×10⁻¹⁴
    • C) Equal to 1×10⁻¹⁴
    • D) Equal to 1×10⁻⁷
  4. For what purpose is the hydrogen-oxygen fuel cell used in spacecraft?
    • A) Power for heat and light
    • B) Power for pressure
    • C) Power for propellation
    • D) Power for uplift
  5. Which of the following indicates lithophone?
    • A) ZnS + BaSO₄
    • B) ZnSO₄ + BaSO₄
    • C) ZnCO₃ + BaS
    • D) ZnS + BaSO₃
  6. Which of the following is the purest form of iron?
    • A) Cast iron
    • B) Steel
    • C) Haematite
    • D) Wrought iron
  7. Nitrobenzene can be prepared from benzene by using a mixture of conc. nitric acid and conc. H₂SO₄ in the nitrating mixture. What is the role of nitric acid?
    • A) A base
    • B) An acid
    • C) A catalyst
    • D) A reducing agent
  8. You are given (I) aniline, (II) o-nitroaniline, (III) p-nitroaniline, and (IV) m-nitroaniline. What is the order of increasing basic nature?
    • A) II < I < III < IV
    • B) I < II < III < IV
    • C) II < III < I < IV
    • D) II < III < IV < I
  9. Which of the following mechanisms can occur in the hydrolysis of isopropyl chloride?
    • A) SN¹ mechanism
    • B) SN² mechanism
    • C) SN¹ and SN² mechanism
    • D) Neither SN¹ nor SN² mechanism
  10. What would be the product when benzaldehyde is heated with conc. NaOH?
    • A) Benzyl alcohol and sodium benzoate
    • B) Sodium benzoate and benzaldehyde
    • C) Sodium benzoate and benzene
    • D) Benzyl alcohol and benzene
  11. A compound 'X' undergoes reduction with LiAlH₄ to yield 'Y'. When 'Y' is passed over copper heated to 300°C, 'X' is formed. What is 'Y'?
    • A) Acetone
    • B) Ethanal
    • C) Ethanol
    • D) Ethoxyethane
